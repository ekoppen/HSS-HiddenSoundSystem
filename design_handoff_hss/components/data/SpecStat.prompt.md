SpecStat — the recurring "label over mono value" readout for hardware/stream stats.

```jsx
<SpecStat label="Stream Bitrate" value="320 kbps" tone="status-success" />
<SpecStat label="DAC Master" value="24-bit / 44.1kHz" tone="accent" />
```

Group 3-4 in a row separated by a 1px divider (see the Now Playing kit) rather than in separate cards.
